Rune Core

Privacy

Credits Privacy Terms

Rune Core is made by Shadreal.

The save stays on your device. It holds your character name, a local id, and your play data. Rune Core does not send that save to Shadreal. There is no account, no ads, and no tracking.

Deleting the app deletes the save on that device.

If you buy something from Google Play or the App Store, that store handles the payment. Rune Core does not take card numbers. Those stores have their own privacy policies.

Contact is in Settings. When you tap Send, the app sends the message you typed, and a reply address only if you typed one, to a Google Form owned by Shadreal. The answers are stored in a Google Sheet and are used only to answer you. The app does not send your character name, local id, save, or device id with that message.

The store listing contact is curiousferret12@gmail.com.
